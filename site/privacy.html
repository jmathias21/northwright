Contact form privacy

About your inquiry.

The contact form asks for your name, email address, product interest, and message. Providing a company name is optional.

How your message is handled

Your submission is processed by Northwright’s contact endpoint hosted on Cloudflare and sent through Resend to our contact email inbox. Cloudflare Turnstile checks for automated abuse before a message is sent. See Cloudflare’s privacy policy and Resend’s privacy policy for their processing practices.

Northwright uses the information you provide to respond to your question and communicate about your product inquiry. Please avoid including passwords or other sensitive information in your message.

Site analytics

This landing page uses Cloudflare for hosting and performance measurement. It does not include advertising trackers. Cloudflare, Turnstile, and Resend may process technical information needed to deliver and protect their services. Northwright does not maintain a separate submissions database for this form; inquiries are handled in our email inbox.
